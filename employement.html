Join our Staff

Aerospace Engineer

Role: Designs, tests, and improves aircraft and spacecraft systems, including propulsion and aerodynamics. Benefits: Competitive salary, access to private research labs, mentorship programs, paid professional certifications, and opportunities to work on next-generation aerospace projects that directly impact innovation.

Material Scientist

Role: Develops advanced materials such as composites and alloys for lightweight, durable, and sustainable aerospace components. Benefits: Research funding support, publication opportunities, flexible scheduling for lab work, and collaboration with international engineering teams focused on sustainability.

Energy Systems Engineer

Role: Focuses on renewable energy integration and efficiency improvements in propulsion and industrial systems. Benefits: Hybrid work options, annual innovation bonuses, travel opportunities for energy conferences, and full health coverage including wellness stipends.

Industrial Design Technician

Role: Works with engineering teams to build prototypes, test equipment, and optimize manufacturing processes. Benefits: Hands-on technical training, overtime pay, safety bonuses, and a clear path to promotion within manufacturing and design management.

Flight Test Specialist

Role: Conducts experimental flight operations, collects performance data, and ensures aircraft safety and compliance. Benefits: Hazard pay for testing conditions, access to exclusive pilot training, company-sponsored travel, and comprehensive insurance including life and disability coverage.
