Aerodynamics &

Propulsion Research

Samson, Arizona – Headquarters (11457 Polk Road, Samson, AZ 85142)

The Samson facility serves as the beating heart of Markenson Industrials, housing our executive offices, primary research divisions, and flagship aerospace testing grounds. Overseeing operations is CEO and founder, Matthew “Matt” Markenson, whose childhood dream of flight and steadfast Christian faith became the cornerstone of the company’s vision. Under his leadership, the Samson headquarters focuses on propulsion research, advanced materials, and the LYFT passenger program. This site is home to the company’s largest wind tunnel and simulation lab, where new aircraft concepts are born and refined. The Arizona desert skies offer ideal testing conditions, symbolizing the vastness of human potential and God’s limitless design.

Houston, Texas – Space Systems Division (3902 Wycliff Avenue, Houston, TX 77057)

The Houston branch of Markenson Industrials specializes in orbital engineering and spacecraft systems development. Led by Chief Space Operations Officer Dr. Hannah Reaves, this location manages satellite deployment, life-support research, and deep-space communication technologies. Houston’s proximity to America’s aerospace corridor provides access to world-class engineers and collaboration with faith-aligned innovators. The team’s mission is to push humanity’s reach beyond Earth while maintaining humility and reverence for the Creator who made the cosmos. Every project launched from Houston represents not just technological progress, but a prayerful step toward exploring God’s vast universe with purpose and integrity.

Denver, Colorado – Aerodynamics and Environmental Research Center

(7813 Ridgeway Parkway, Denver, CO 80239)

Nestled at the edge of the Rocky Mountains, Markenson Industrials’ Denver facility focuses on environmental flight systems and aerodynamic optimization. Directed by Chief Engineering Officer Lucas Bennett, this sector studies sustainable aircraft propulsion, atmospheric testing, and eco-efficient design. Bennett’s team is driven by a simple belief—that innovation should honor creation, not harm it. Denver’s altitude and weather conditions make it an ideal location for high-altitude simulation and climate-responsive testing. Together, the engineers, researchers, and technicians at this site continue to craft technologies that elevate performance while protecting the world beneath the wings of every Markenson craft.
