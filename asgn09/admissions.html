Visiting Martian University.

We love that you would like to come to our University! Our university begins taking applications for students the year before attendance, and we are highly selective of the people that we let into our university. Our current acceptance rate is <1%, with over 100,000 applicants each year, of which only 550 students are selected to come to our school.

You can tour our school once a year, with the corporation SpaceX, for the low price of $45,000. This covers both the tour fee, and the round trip flight for you and one other person. This College For a Weekend, or CFAW, will give you an idea of what it is like to attend our University, and even let you sit in some classes!
