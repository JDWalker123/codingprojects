Markenson Industrials is a Christian-based aerospace engineering firm built on faith, innovation, and the belief that all things are possible through God. Founded with a vision to honor Christ through excellence in engineering, the company combines cutting-edge aerospace technology with strong moral values and a commitment to integrity. The story of Markenson Industrials began not in a corporate office, but in the heart of a child who dreamed of touching the stars. From a young age, the founder was fascinated with flight—building model rockets, sketching aircraft, and imagining what lay beyond Earth’s horizon. That dream only grew stronger with time, eventually leading to a bold decision: to leave college and pursue the calling that God had placed on his heart. It wasn’t an easy road—there were setbacks, long nights, and plenty of uncertainty—but through prayer, perseverance, and unwavering faith, the vision became reality. Today, Markenson Industrials stands as a testament to what can happen when faith and determination come together. Every aircraft, satellite, and innovation produced by the company serves not only as a leap in technology but also as a symbol of how following God’s plan can lift dreams beyond the clouds.
